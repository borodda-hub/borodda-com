**Most of a journey by air is spent sitting still, in a room no one set out to design.**

The departure lounge is where an airline's operations, an airport's architecture and a traveler's patience meet. This IPRO studio, run in summer 2021 with United Airlines and the Infosys Strategic Consulting Group, asked students to treat that room as a design problem in its own right.

Teams drawn from across Illinois Tech studied the lounge as a service, a space and an information environment at once, and developed proposals for what it could become.

The challenge was scope. A gate area belongs to many owners and answers to safety, schedule and revenue before comfort. Learning to design inside those constraints, with partners who live with them, was the point of the studio.

The studio was part of [IPRO Labs](/projects/ipro-labs/).
