**How do you exhibit an instrument that almost no one will ever see?**

The South Pole Telescope studies the oldest light in the universe from one of the least accessible places on Earth. Manifest/Space was a graduate studio at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago that set design students the problem of making that instrument, and the science it serves, present to a public audience.

The studio was developed with the Kavli Institute for Cosmological Physics at the University of Chicago, the Adler Planetarium and Argonne National Laboratory. Students designed and fabricated the exhibition themselves.

The difficulty was translation in both directions. Scientists had to explain work whose evidence is statistical and invisible; designers had to find physical form for it without turning it into decoration. The studio treated the exhibition as a research question and the fabrication shop as the place it would be answered.

It was an early version of the partnered studio model that later shaped [IPRO Labs](/projects/ipro-labs/) at Illinois Tech.
