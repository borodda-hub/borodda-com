**When the data about an emergency is incomplete, what should a map of it be allowed to claim?**

Between 2017 and 2019, a sequence of IPRO studios at Illinois Tech worked with the American Red Cross and the Motorola Solutions Foundation on a single question: what could analysis and visualization do for the people who prepare for and respond to fire emergencies and other disasters?

The studios asked students to look for the places where a better way of seeing response data could change a decision.

The challenge was humility about the data. Emergency records are incomplete by nature, and a confident map of partial information can mislead the people who rely on it. The studios treated that limit as part of the design brief.

The work connects the [Visualization as Humanistic Medium](/research/visualization-as-humanistic-medium/) strand to the partnered teaching that became [IPRO Labs](/projects/ipro-labs/).
