**A toy is a design problem with nowhere to hide: it either gets played with or it does not.**

The Big Monster Toys Lab was an IPRO studio at Illinois Tech run in partnership with Big Monster Toys, the Chicago toy invention firm. It asked what students from engineering, computing, architecture and the humanities learn when the brief is play.

Toy design compresses the whole of a design process into something small enough to hold. An idea has to be prototyped, handed to someone else, and watched. The studio used that loop, repeated quickly, as its method.

The challenge was teaching making to students who did not think of themselves as makers, and teaching judgment about play to students trained to optimize. Reflections on the studio were presented at the IndieCade Horizons Educators' Symposium in 2025.

The lab was part of [IPRO Labs](/projects/ipro-labs/).
