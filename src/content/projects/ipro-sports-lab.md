**How does an athlete decide that a piece of equipment is right?**

The IPRO Sports Lab began with that question, asked alongside Wilson Sporting Goods. A fitting is a decision made under uncertainty, with the body as the instrument and feel as the evidence. The partnership set out to study that experience and to ask where new technology could make it better informed.

The work ran as sponsored research on the experience of golf fittings and as a sequence of IPRO studios across 2022 and 2023, including a VR and E-Sports Lab with VR Motion. The studios were set up for students to research that experience and prototype responses to it.

The challenge was that the subject resists measurement. What a player reports and what a sensor records are not the same thing, and a good fitting has to honor both.

The partnership was one of the foundations of the Center for Sports Innovation at Illinois Tech, and part of [IPRO Labs](/projects/ipro-labs/).
