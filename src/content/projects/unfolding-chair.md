**What is left of a folding chair when it no longer folds?**

The steel folding chair is furniture at its most provisional: cheap, stackable, brought out and put away. The Unfolding Folding Chair, by [ChiLab Design Studio](https://chilabstudio.com), takes that object and casts it solid in bronze.

The piece is a short argument about value and use. The form stays recognizable, and almost everything else about the object changes: its weight, its cost, its permanence, the way a person approaches it. It asks how much of what we think of as design lives in the shape of a thing, and how much in the material and labor that stand behind it.

The chair was first shown with ChiLab at Wanted Design in New York in 2013, then at Tent London and in *CHGO DSGN: Recent Object and Graphic Design* at the Chicago Cultural Center in 2014. It is a companion to the [Boolean Lamp](/projects/boolean-lamp/), which makes a related move with a clip light and blown glass.
